What a strong answer should do

    Practice goal

    Answer the question out loud before reading the coaching. Aim for a clear example instead of a perfect script.

    Answer help

    Interview reminders

    • Use specific examples when you can.
    • Explain what you did, not only what the team did.
    • Connect your answer back to students and learning.
    • Keep most responses around 1–2 minutes unless they ask for more detail.
