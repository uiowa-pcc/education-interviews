Prepare for a Teacher Interview

Build a small story bank you can reuse across different questions. Focus on what you actually did, why you made that choice, and what happened next.

Step 1 of 4

Start with the role

You do not need to paste the full posting. Look for repeated themes such as collaboration, differentiation, family communication, literacy, data use, or classroom culture.

Step 2 of 4

Choose the examples worth preparing

Select 4–6 topics that are most likely to matter for this role. You can prepare more later.

Step 3 of 4

Build your story bank

Student teaching, practicum, tutoring, coaching, camps, youth programs, jobs, leadership, and other experiences can all provide useful evidence.

Step 4 of 4

Your interview prep sheet

Use this as talking points—not a script. In the interview, answer the actual question and choose the example that fits best.
