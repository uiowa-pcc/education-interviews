    How to use this practice

    1. Read the question.
    2. Start the timer and answer it out loud on your own.
    3. Then open the coaching and resources.
    4. Try the question again if you want to improve your response.

    Interview reminders

    • Use specific examples when you can.
    • Explain what you did, not only what the team did.
    • Connect your answer back to students and learning.
    • Keep most responses around 1–2 minutes unless they ask for more detail.
